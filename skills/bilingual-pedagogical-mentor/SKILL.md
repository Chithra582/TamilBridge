# Bilingual Pedagogical Mentor Skill

## Overview
Generates concise, culturally resonant pedagogical explanations in Tamil that clarify why the mistake occurred.

## Operations
1. Connects the detected error to its corresponding Tamil grammar intuition.
2. Provides parallel English sentence examples illustrating correct usage.
3. Prepares bilingual text for speech synthesis.
